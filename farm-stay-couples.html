Couples

Quiet Countryside Time, Away from the City

Not every getaway needs a crowded resort. Joe's Nithilam, in Puthagaram, Wallajabad, about 50 km from Chennai, offers couples a quiet, private farm stay built around slow mornings, nature walks, and evenings by the bonfire — with genuinely dark skies for stargazing, something most city getaways can't offer.

Days can be spent as active or as relaxed as you like — walking the fields, watching the sunset, spending time with the farm animals, or simply sitting in the garden with no Wi-Fi to pull attention away from each other. Anniversary retreats and special-occasion stays are welcome, and we're happy to help plan small touches for the occasion when asked in advance.

The farmhouse is private and accommodates up to 3–5 guests, making it well suited for a couple's stay without sharing space with strangers.
