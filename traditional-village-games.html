Traditional Games

Games Your Grandparents Played, Brought Back to Life

Long before video games, villages across Tamil Nadu played games that needed nothing but a few seeds, stones, a rope, or an open field. At Joe's Nithilam, those traditional games are still part of everyday farm life — offered to guests, school groups, and day-trip visitors alongside hands-on farming activities.

Depending on the visit and season, guests can play Pallanguzhi, Sottanguli (Five Stones), Paramapadam, Nondi, Kitti Pul, Uriyadi, Thaayam, Aadu Puli Aattam, Kannaamoochi, Kokku Parakkudhu, Pambaram, and Kayiru Izhuthal. These pair naturally with farm activities like sowing seeds, watering plants, and harvesting vegetables — making a visit as much about culture and play as it is about farming.

It's a favourite for school farm visits and family outings alike, since the games work for a wide range of ages and group sizes with very little setup.

Board & Seed Games

Pallanguzhi, Sottanguli, Paramapadam, and Thaayam — classic games played with seeds, shells, or dice.

Outdoor & Active Games

Nondi, Kitti Pul, Uriyadi, Kokku Parakkudhu, and Kannaamoochi for open-field play.

Paired with Farm Activities

Games sit alongside real farming — sowing, watering, and harvesting — for a complete rural experience.
