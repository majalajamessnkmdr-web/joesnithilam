Celebrations

Celebrate Somewhere the City Can't Offer

A birthday or anniversary at Joe's Nithilam looks nothing like a hall booking — think farm animals instead of balloons stands, a bonfire instead of a banquet, and a star-filled sky instead of string lights. Located in Puthagaram, Wallajabad, about 50 km from Chennai, it's close enough for guests to travel in for the day or stay overnight.

We can accommodate birthday celebrations, anniversary retreats, and family gatherings in the farm setting, with home-cooked South Indian meals and the usual farm activities — animal interaction, traditional games, nature walks, and the pumpset and tank — built into the occasion.

Since the farmhouse comfortably fits 3–5 guests, celebrations here tend to suit close family or small-group gatherings rather than large parties. Reach out in advance so we can plan the arrangements around your date and group size.
