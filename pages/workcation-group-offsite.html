Workcation & Small Group Offsite Near Chennai

Joe's Nithilam hosts small teams and groups for a workcation or offsite near Chennai — a quiet, nature-first setting about 50 km from the city.

Workcation

Swap the Conference Room for Open Fields

For small teams looking for a change of scenery rather than a full-blown resort conference package, Joe's Nithilam offers a farm-based workcation and offsite setting in Puthagaram, Wallajabad, Kanchipuram district — about 50 km from Chennai, close enough for a day trip or an overnight offsite.

The setting suits informal planning sessions, team bonding, or simply a few focused days away from the office, with farm activities — nature walks, traditional games, bonfires, and the pumpset and tank — available as breaks between work sessions. Note that Wi-Fi is not currently available at the farm, so it's better suited to teams planning a genuine break or working with mobile data/hotspots.

Small group outings can be arranged subject to the size of the group and accommodation — please contact us in advance to plan logistics for your team.
