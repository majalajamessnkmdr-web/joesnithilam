Farm Day Trip for Kids Near Chennai

Bring the kids for a single-day farm outing near Chennai — planting, traditional games, and snacks, without booking an overnight stay.

Day Trips

A Full Day of Farm Fun, No Overnight Stay Needed

Not every family visit needs an overnight stay — Joe's Nithilam welcomes day visits too, which makes it an easy farm day trip for kids near Chennai. In a single day, children get a real taste of farm life: a guided farm tour, hands-on farming activities, and simple workshops, rounded off with traditional games and refreshments.

The farm is about 50 km from Chennai, around 1 to 1.10 hours by road, making it comfortable to visit and return the same day. It suits birthday outings, weekend family plans, small playgroups, or simply an alternative to another mall visit — kids get fresh air, open fields, and games instead of screens.

Because activities depend on the season and farm conditions, we recommend messaging us ahead of your visit so we can plan the day around what's available.

Nature Walks

Kids explore the paddy fields and gardens, spotting birds, butterflies, and plants along the way.

Traditional Games

Pallanguzhi, Nondi, Uriyadi, Pambaram, and other village games kids rarely get to play today.

Snacks & Refreshments

Simple snacks and refreshments included as part of the day-visit experience.
