A Countryside Farm Stay Near Chennai

Just 50 km from Chennai, Joe's Nithilam offers a genuine farmhouse stay surrounded by paddy fields, farm animals, and open sky in Puthagaram, Wallajabad.

Farm Stay

Escape the City to an Authentic Farm Stay

Looking for a farm stay near Chennai that's more than just a resort with a garden? Joe's Nithilam is a working farm in Puthagaram, Wallajabad, Kanchipuram district, Tamil Nadu — about 50 km from Chennai and roughly 1 to 1.10 hours by road. It's close enough for a spontaneous weekend, yet far enough to feel like a proper countryside escape.

The farmhouse comfortably accommodates 3–5 guests, making it ideal for a family trip, a couple's retreat, or a small friends' getaway. Days here are unhurried — feed the farm animals, walk the fields, cool off at the pumpset and tank, and end the evening with a bonfire under a genuinely dark, star-filled sky.

Unlike a conventional resort, Joe's Nithilam is built around real farm life — sowing, watering, and harvesting alongside the family that runs the farm, home-cooked South Indian meals, and no Wi-Fi to pull you back to the city.
