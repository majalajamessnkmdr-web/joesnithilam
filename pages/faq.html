Joe's Nithilam is a farm stay and rural farm experience in Puthagaram, Wallajabad, Kanchipuram district, Tamil Nadu. Guests can experience farming, nature walks, traditional games, outdoor activities, stargazing, bonfires, and a peaceful countryside environment.

Joe's Nithilam is located at Puthagaram, Wallajabad, Kanchipuram district, Tamil Nadu — approximately 50 km from Chennai and around 1 to 1.10 hours by road, depending on traffic.

Check-in is at 10:00 AM and check-out is at 09:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged on request, subject to availability.

There is 1 farmhouse that comfortably accommodates up to 3-5 guests. Larger groups and special events can be discussed separately.

We can buy or arrange food for you on request, and Swiggy and Zomato also deliver to the farm.

Yes. Day visits include a farm tour, farming activities, hands-on workshops, traditional games, and refreshments. School visits include a guided farm tour, farming demonstrations, planting, and nature activities.

Depending on the visit and season: Pallanguzhi, Sottanguli (Five Stones), Paramapadam, Nondi, Kitti Pul, Uriyadi, Thaayam, Aadu Puli Aattam, Kannaamoochi, Kokku Parakkudhu, Pambaram, and Kayiru Izhuthal.

There is a traditional pumpset and tank experience rather than a conventional resort swimming pool. Water activities should always be undertaken with adult supervision.

Yes, well-behaved pets are welcome. Guests should inform Joe's Nithilam while booking so appropriate arrangements can be made.

Yes. Children love farming, nature walks, outdoor and traditional Tamil games, and the pumpset and tank with adult supervision. Children below 5 years stay free.

Yes. Small group outings, workcations, corporate offsites, and family reunions can be accommodated, subject to group size and arrangements agreed in advance.

Wi-Fi is currently not available at the farm, allowing guests to disconnect for a digital detox. Mobile network coverage is available for most operators.

Cancellations 7+ days before check-in receive a full refund. Cancellations 3–7 days before receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 3 days are non-refundable.

Joe's Nithilam focuses on an authentic working-farm experience rather than a conventional resort stay, with hands-on farming, nature, and traditional games alongside a relaxed, unplugged countryside stay.

Guests can contact Joe's Nithilam directly through WhatsApp or phone to check availability and discuss accommodation, day visits, school trips, celebrations, or group arrangements.
